# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Preflight is infrastructure that governs the boundary between AI agents and the real world. The community building it must reflect the same standards of rigor, accountability, and respect that the software demands.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, professional environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

## Standards

### Expected Behavior

- Use clear, precise, professional language
- Respect differing viewpoints and technical approaches
- Accept constructive criticism with professionalism
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users
- Show empathy and consideration toward other community members
- Credit others' work and ideas

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or contributions
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, community channels, and any public space where an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at conduct@preflight.dev. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially.

Project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
